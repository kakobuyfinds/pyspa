From a find to an informed decision

How to use this LitBuy spreadsheet guide

Build a useful shortlist

  1. Identify the exact listing.

    Save the item ID, product name and original seller URL. Similar photographs can appear across different listings.

  2. Confirm the selected option.

    Record the color, size, model and quantity. A starting price may refer to one piece rather than the complete outfit shown.

  3. Check whether your agent accepts it.

    If you plan to use LitBuy, obtain the original marketplace link and confirm that the exact listing can be purchased there before paying.

  4. Keep an order record.

    Save payment details, the seller’s return terms, inspection photos and parcel information alongside the listing reference.

Check the full cost

A product price is one part of the budget. Compare every cost in the same currency and use the selected variant’s current price.

LitBuy spreadsheet questions

What is a LitBuy spreadsheet?

A LitBuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize finds, listing links, prices, sizes and order notes when planning purchases through LitBuy. This independent page combines a buying guide with a selection sourced from HipobuyFind.

Where do the Buy it now buttons go?

All Buy it now buttons on this page open https://hipobuyfind.com. They do not open LitBuy checkout. Find the matching item ID on HipobuyFind and check its current listing.

Do the displayed prices include shipping?

No. The displayed CNY amounts are imported listing references. Domestic delivery, agent charges, international shipping, currency conversion charges and any applicable taxes must be checked separately.

Are product listing images QC photos?

No. These are source catalog images, not inspection evidence for your order. Check the photos and measurements supplied for your actual warehouse item before authorizing shipment.

Is this an official LitBuy website?

No. This is an independent research page. It is not operated by LitBuy and does not provide LitBuy accounts, checkout, customer support or shipping services.

Can I download the product references?

Yes. Select Save on the items you want, then use Export saved finds to download a CSV containing their IDs, names, categories, imported CNY prices and source URLs. The shortlist stays in this browser when local storage is available.

How current is the catalog?

The eight references were imported on 19 November 2026. This date describes the catalog snapshot, not a purchase, authentication, stock check or inspection of each product. Confirm the current options and prices on the source site.
